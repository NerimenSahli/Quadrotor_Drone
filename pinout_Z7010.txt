Pmod JC - I2C + MPU6050 interrupt
----------------------------------
JC Pin | FPGA Pin | Signal            | Notes
JC1    | V15      | IIC_0_scl_io      | SCL, internal pull-up enabled
JC2    | W15      | IIC_0_sda_io      | SDA, internal pull-up enabled
JC3    | T11      | mpu6050_int[0]    | INT pin from MPU6050
JC6    | -        | VCC (3.3V)        |
JC5    | -        | GND               |


Pmod JD - Motor control lines
------------------------------
JD Pin | FPGA Pin | Signal
JD1    | T14      | mot_a
JD2    | T15      | mot_b
JD3    | P14      | mot_c
JD4    | R14      | mot_d
JD6    | -        | VCC (3.3V)
JD5    | -        | GND


Pmod JA - UART
---------------
JA Pin | FPGA Pin | Signal
JA1    | N15      | uart_rtl_rxd
JA2    | L14      | uart_rtl_txd
